# Community Code of Conduct

Frederick Radius wants public discussion to be useful, local, and safe.

## Expected

- Address the idea or product behavior, not the person.
- Be clear about what you observed and what you inferred.
- Correct mistakes without piling on.
- Respect privacy and the sensitivity of emergency, health, family, and location information.
- Make room for people with different abilities, backgrounds, experience, and points of view.

## Not accepted

- harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual attention, or personal attacks;
- publishing or requesting private or identifying information;
- impersonation, spam, coordinated disruption, or deceptive promotion;
- unverified accusations about a person or organization; or
- repeated behavior that makes participation unsafe or unproductive.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions; limit participation; or block accounts when needed to protect the community and the project. Moderation decisions consider context, impact, and patterns of behavior.

To report conduct privately, email **hello@frederickradius.app** with the subject `Community conduct`. Include links and a concise description. Do not repost harmful content unnecessarily.

This code applies to this repository and to project spaces moderated by Frederick Radius.
