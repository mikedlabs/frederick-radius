# Contributing

Frederick Radius is built for people who live in, work in, and visit Frederick County. Clear feedback from real use is valuable.

## Choose the right path

- Use the bug form when something is broken, misleading, inaccessible, or difficult to use.
- Use the feature form when a new capability would solve a specific local need.
- Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities. Never report them in public.
- Use the contact route in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) for account, privacy, partnership, or licensing questions.

Before filing a new issue, search open and closed issues for the same problem.

## Write a useful issue

A strong report includes:

- the page or feature involved;
- what you expected and what happened instead;
- simple steps that reproduce the problem;
- the device and browser, when relevant; and
- the town or general area only when location affects the result.

Screenshots are welcome after removing names, addresses, notification contents, account details, and other personal information.

## Local facts and corrections

For a correction to a place, event, route, deal, or public notice, include a direct source that Frederick Radius can verify. An official page or first-party announcement is best. Do not paste a copied database, private group post, paywalled article, or personal record.

A report is a lead, not confirmation. Frederick Radius may label, verify, defer, or decline a change based on source quality, freshness, relevance, and publication rights.

## Pull requests

The production application is maintained in a private repository. Unsolicited production-code pull requests are not accepted here.

Small documentation pull requests may be accepted when a maintainer has requested one in an issue. Keep the change focused and do not add generated content, dependencies, binaries, datasets, images, workflows, analytics, tracking, or external scripts.

By contributing, you confirm that you have the right to submit the material and agree to the contribution terms in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Community expectations

Be specific, patient, and respectful. Read [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before participating.
