# Frederick Radius Facebook launch

Frederick Radius Brand System 1.2 - July 22, 2026

This is the working copy and setup sheet for the Frederick Radius Facebook
Page. Keep the Page independent, useful, and easy to understand. Do not use a
government category or imply that Frederick County, the City of Frederick, or
another municipality endorses the app.

## Page setup

- Page name: `Frederick Radius`
- Username: `@FrederickRadius`
- Primary category: choose `Local service` or the closest available local-guide
  category. Do not use `Government organization`.
- Website: `https://frederickradius.app/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=page`
- Action button: use `Learn more` or `Visit website`, whichever Facebook offers.
  Do not use `Sign up`; people can try the beta without creating an account.
- Email: `hello@frederickradius.app`
- Address: use the business PO box only if Facebook requires a public mailing
  address. Never publish a home address.
- Service area: Frederick County, Maryland
- Hours: leave blank. Radius is a website, not a storefront.

## Bio

## About

> Frederick Radius is an independent guide to Frederick County. It organizes
> nearby businesses and current events by location, so you do not have to search
> separate sites. The map also includes parking information and public amenities
> when those details help you get around. Coverage starts in Downtown Frederick
> and extends across the county's municipalities.
>
> I have spent more than a year building and refining Radius. It combines public
> sources and business information with details I gathered in the field,
> including amenities I mapped by hand. The site is still in beta, so I want to
> hear when a result is wrong.
>
> Frederick Radius is not affiliated with or endorsed by the City of Frederick,
> Frederick County Government, or any municipality.

## First week

1. Publish and pin the Page launch post.
2. Share the community-group version from your personal profile, not as a
   polished ad from the Page.
3. Post one current Today-page example when the information is genuinely useful.
4. Show one hand-mapped detail and explain why the dataset did not already exist.
5. Ask one narrow question: `What did Radius miss near you?`

Do not publish a string of generic feature lists. Show one real Frederick use
case per post and link to the exact page that handles it.

## Tracking and review

Keep the `utm_source=facebook` links above. In Plausible, compare Facebook
referrals with visits to Today, Map, Ask, and Food trucks. Review whether those
visits reach a useful action instead of judging the launch only by likes.

Before posting a time-sensitive claim, open the linked page in a private window
and test the beta code. Verify every featured event, open status, location, and
business link on the day it is published.
