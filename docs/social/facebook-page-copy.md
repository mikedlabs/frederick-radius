# Frederick Radius — Facebook Page build (rev. 2)

Revised to center the city-and-county connection and drop "signal" and "a living field guide." Nothing is published yet.

## Bio (short line under the name)
> Downtown Frederick and the county, connected. What's open, what's on, and what's worth your time, right now.

## Description
> Frederick Radius connects Downtown Frederick, the city, and the surrounding towns into one place. It helps you find what's open, what's happening, and what's actually worth your time nearby, right now. Not another restaurant list or events calendar: food, events, parks, shops, public spaces, local info, and the hidden gems in between, curated instead of cluttered. Premium, local, and very Frederick. In beta.

## Our Story — "Why Frederick Radius"
> I've lived in Downtown Frederick for years, and I love this place. There's so much happening here, but it's still too easy to miss.
>
> Frederick Radius is my attempt to fix that: to make the county feel more connected, easier to navigate, and more alive. Downtown and the towns around it, pulled into one place that answers the question you actually have. What should I do, where should I go, and what's worth my time right now?
>
> Part guide, part map, part local knowledge. It's in beta, and it's built to feel useful and local, not cluttered.
