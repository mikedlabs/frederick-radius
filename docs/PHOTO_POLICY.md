# Frederick Radius photo policy

> **Amendment, 2026-10-07 (recorded 2026-10-08).** Where this policy
> conflicts with [`docs/VISUAL_FIRST.md`](VISUAL_FIRST.md), VISUAL_FIRST wins.
> Event rows follow its events ladder: the publisher's flyer, then a credited
> venue photo, then the venue's map tile only when the event has a precise
> geocode, and otherwise the date plate. A map tile is not a photo. One part of
> this waits on the owner: whether the dense event rows may carry a flyer or a
> venue photo (see "Dense event rows" below).

**Status:** Owner-approved 2026-06-06. Amended 2026-06-16, 2026-07-07 and
2026-10-07. "Rules in force" and "Dense event rows" are in force except where
they say "Pending owner call". "How /events implements this today" describes
the current code and is not a rule.
**Scope:** Where photography may and may not appear. VISUAL_FIRST owns the
image ladder, missing-photo fallbacks and the visual-floor check. This page
keeps only the placement rules that the ladder does not cover.
**History:** The full earlier record, with its June audit figures, the
typographic-default principle and the phased photo-removal plan, is in
[`docs/archive/2026-06/PHOTO_POLICY-2026-06.md`](archive/2026-06/PHOTO_POLICY-2026-06.md).
It is history, not instructions, and nothing in it gates code.

## Rules in force

- **Category and intent tiles stay typographic.** The craving tiles
  (`src/components/now/CravingStrip.tsx`), category filter chips and lane
  doorways name a category, not a business, so no single photo is honest
  there. They keep their glyphs. (2026-06-16)
- **Town heroes are curated only.** A town hero is a verified, credited town
  photo (`TOWN_PHOTOS` in `src/lib/integrations/wikimedia.ts`) or owner
  photography (`SeasonalPhoto`), never a random place's imported photo. See
  `src/app/(app)/m/[municipality]/page.tsx`. (2026-06-06)
- **Suppression stays.** A shared, wrong or mis-geocoded photo is removed and
  the place falls to the next rung of the ladder, because no photo is better
  than the wrong photo. The de-twin list `src/data/photo-suppress.json` is
  generated by `npm run build:photo-suppress` (issue #437), and per-place
  `clearPhoto` patches live in `src/data/places-overrides.json`. Both apply in
  `src/lib/loaders/places.ts`. (2026-06-06)
- **"Looks like {town}" stays gated.** A photo wall for a town may appear
  only if its pool is limited to curated or controlled images. Otherwise the
  module hides. `PhotoMosaic` draws from Google photos and has no caller
  today (`grep -rn PhotoMosaic src`), so re-adding it needs that gate first.
  (2026-06-06)
- **One photo lead per events horizon group.** On /events, each horizon
  group's lead may carry one approved image, so a group's lead shows at most
  one photograph. A lead without an approved image does not get an enlarged
  glyph in its place (see the last rule below). The rows under a lead are
  covered by "Dense event rows". (2026-07-07)
- **One image per page claims the LCP preload on /events.** Only one event
  image on the page sets the next/image `priority` preload, and every other
  event image loads lazily, because competing preloads slow the real LCP. The
  flag is `priorityImage` on `EventCard`. (2026-07-07)
- **Venue photos are borrowed only through trusted matches.**
  `withVenueThumbs` in `src/lib/loaders/eventThumb.ts` gives an event its
  venue's photo through the shared venue resolver (canonical slug, a reviewed
  alias in `REVIEWED_EVENT_VENUE_ALIASES` in
  `src/lib/events/venue-resolver.ts`, or a unique exact name). For precisely
  geocoded events only, it also accepts a name match within 300 m. A borrowed
  photo without its credit record is dropped, and `eventCardVisual` in
  `src/components/event/eventVisuals.ts` approves what a card may show. The
  /events/[slug] resolver runs the same borrow (`withVenueThumb`), so the
  detail hero matches the list card. Add an alias only after a person
  confirms the venue, because aliases also move pins. (2026-07-07)
- **Photos stay on detail pages.** Place and event pages show their photo
  when one loads, following the ladder, and every imported photo there stays
  suppressible by the mechanisms above. (2026-06-06)
- **No oversized glyph plate as ornament.** Do not enlarge a category glyph to
  fill an empty photo slot. A large frame without a photo falls to the map
  block or the date plate, and VISUAL_FIRST keeps the category mark for small
  frames. (2026-07-07)

## Dense event rows

The compact, utility and agenda rows on /events (the `compact` and `utility`
variants of `src/components/event/EventCard.tsx` and
`src/components/event/EventAgenda.tsx`) may carry the venue's map tile beside
the date plate now, when the event's geocode is precise (`isGeoPrecise` in
`src/lib/events/geo-confidence.ts`). A map tile is real geography, not a
photo, so it breaks neither this policy nor VISUAL_FIRST.

Pending owner call: may compact, utility and agenda event rows carry a flyer
thumbnail or a credited venue photo beside the date plate, not only a map tile
for venues with a precise geocode? VISUAL_FIRST says every row carries one,
while the 2026-06-16 and 2026-07-07 amendments kept these rows photoless.
Until the owner answers, add no photographs to these rows.

## How /events implements this today

This section describes the code at the time of writing. It is not a rule, and
nothing here needs an amendment to change.

Today only the first horizon group leads with the poster card.
`horizonLeadVariant` in `src/components/event/eventsExplorerLayout.ts` returns
`"feature"` for the first group and `"glance"` for every later one.
`EventsExplorer.tsx` renders that lead through `PromotedEvent`, which is the
only feature card on the page and the one event image that sets
`priorityImage`. A glance lead shows a 72 px lazy thumbnail only when
`eventCardVisual` approves an image. That is narrower than the 2026-07-07 rule
allows. The rules above limit how many photographs a lead carries and which
image claims the preload. They do not require the poster card, the glance card
or any particular lead layout. VISUAL_FIRST asks each events section to lead
with flyers shown whole, so a change to the lead layout in that direction is
consistent with this page.

## No longer in force

These earlier rules were overturned and are kept only in the archive.

- Type-only cards as the default for browse, list, recommendation, map, saved
  and event surfaces (2026-06-06). VISUAL_FIRST makes a loaded photo the
  first rung.
- Detail-page photos as "supplemental, not the spine of the page"
  (2026-06-06). A place page's hero is its photo or its block on the map.
- No borrowed venue photos or feed imagery on event cards, and a Today event
  lead with no image (2026-06-06).
- "No code until the plan is approved" and the photo-removal Phases 1 and 2
  (2026-06-06). The plan they guarded was reversed.
- Compact, utility and agenda event rows stay photoless (2026-06-16,
  2026-07-07). Map tiles may go there now, and photographs wait on the
  pending owner call above.
