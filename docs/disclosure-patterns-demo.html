2 · Two-stage peek sheet

A place card opens to a short peek — name, open status, the 3 actions — so you can triage fast. Drag the handle (or tap “Full details”) to commit to the whole record. This is the upgrade over your current single-height sheet.

3 · Drill-in funnel

Broad need → narrowed answer. Each tap reveals the next set and hides the last. The reverse (Back) is always one tap. ≤2 taps to a ranked answer.
