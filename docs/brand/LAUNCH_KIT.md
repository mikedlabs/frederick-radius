# Frederick Radius launch kit

Frederick Radius Brand System 1.2 - July 22, 2026

This kit is for the public beta. The public name is Frederick Radius. Its
tagline is `Frederick County starts where you are.` Its plain-language
description is `Current local information for Frederick County, organized
around where you are.` Keep the writing specific. Do not describe Radius as an
official service, and do not imply that a source or municipality endorses it.

programming, and proofing.

## Brand consistency before export

- Use Public Sans for product and campaign titles, captions, details, dates,
  and calls to action. The package includes variable upright and real italic
  files. Use Libre Caslon Display Regular only for the wordmark or a rare large
  editorial or campaign headline; never synthesize a bold or italic Caslon.
- Keep Brick, Cream, Ink, the Ripple, and owned Frederick photography as the
  recognition system. Catoctin Forest is a restrained outdoor or landscape
  data cue, not an alternate campaign theme.
- Use Catoctin Forest for an open-state label. Use Ochre Amber only when the
  post describes something live, cautionary, related to sunlight, or
  specifically about beer. In beer material, it can
  mark an active taste choice, a flagship beer, or beer and brewery metadata.
  Do not use it as a general yellow decoration or a full campaign theme.
- Use one solid Ripple at the correct optical size. Opacity is only for
  background texture or motion. Do not add a location pin,
  initials, or another logo treatment.
- Check the final crop at the actual phone size. A layout that works on the
  editable master can still fail when a platform trims its edges.
- Add useful alt text that names the photographed place and describes what is
  visible. Do not repeat the caption word for word.

## Before anything is printed or pitched

- Confirm the beta URL and access code in a private browser window.
- Test every QR code from a physical proof. Keep the readable URL beside it.
- Verify the Privacy and Terms pages, including the email-use explanation.
- Keep the independent-project disclaimer visible in the footer and fact sheet.
- Remove EXIF and precise GPS metadata from public photo derivatives.
- Check every featured business name, town, open status, and link on the day of
  publication.
- Keep third-party data attribution attached to the information it supports.
- Confirm that safety language never replaces 911 or an official source.
- Prepare one phone screenshot each for Today, Map, Events, and Ask Radius.
- Set a real person and monitored inbox for press and correction requests.
- Make sure error monitoring, uptime alerts, and the correction form work.
- Save the final copy, image source, rights note, and publish date for every
  campaign asset.
- Export from the current SVG master and compare it with the live app header,
  avatar, and share card before publishing a campaign set.
- Rebuild the app icons after any Ripple or core-color change, then bump the
  icon version in `src/lib/platform-brand.ts` so browsers, installed apps, and
  the service worker request the new files.
- Treat a rebuilt SVG, PNG, icon, or PDF as local until its commit is deployed
  and the public URL has been checked. A successful export is not evidence that
  production has changed.

## Public-response rule

Answer with the actual fix, source, or limitation. Do not argue about AI. Do
not treat every comment as a product requirement, but investigate specific
claims that can be tested. If something is still wrong, say so plainly and put
it on the beta list.
